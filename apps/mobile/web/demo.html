Local Everywhere!

Your money travels acrros Africa. We connect it to local payment rails wherever you go!

Millions of visitors and diaspora travellers arrive in Africa with international cards and bank accounts, but everyday payments often depend on local mobile-money ecosystems.

VessPay bridges that gap.

You load your wallet using the money you already have, and vessPAY lets you pay local mobile-money users in their local currency without requiring you to buy a local SIM.

USD/GBP/EUR → GH₵ / NGN | MoMo / Bank

How it works

  1. Fund a wallet in USD/GBP/EUR. Bank transfer or crypto, settled into the wallet.
  2. Enter any MoMo number. No local SIM and no MoMo registration required.
  3. vessPAY pays out. Straight to MTN, Telecel or any bank.
