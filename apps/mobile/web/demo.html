VessPay

Pay in Africa without a local SIM.

Alex lands in Accra with no Ghanaian SIM, no Mobile Money account and no cash. He needs to pay a taxi driver GH₵150.

Today that means hunting for an ATM, an exchange desk, or a friend with a MoMo wallet.

How it works

  1. Fund a wallet in USD. Bank transfer or crypto, settled into the wallet.
  2. Enter any MoMo number. No local SIM and no MoMo registration required.
  3. VessPay converts and pays out. Straight to MTN, Telecel or a Ghanaian bank.
